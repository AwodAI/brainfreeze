3. The tool brainfreeze-poster

brainfreeze-poster is an internal tool. It uploads short videos that we produce ourselves to our own channels and reads basic statistics of those uploads. It has no public users; only the owner of the channels signs in and authorizes it.

  • YouTube API Services: the tool uploads videos to our own channel (scope youtube.upload) and reads the channel name and the view and like counts of our own uploads (scope youtube.readonly). It does not access data of any other user, channel or viewer.
  • Stored data: only video IDs, post links, upload time and view/like counts of our own uploads, in a log file on our own computer. Access tokens are stored locally, readable only by the owner. Nothing is stored on a server.
  • Sharing: we do not sell, share or transfer this data to third parties.
  • Storage period: view and like counts are refreshed from the API at least every 30 days or deleted. If access is revoked, all stored YouTube data of that account is deleted within 7 days; it is also deleted at any time on request.

By using YouTube API Services you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service; Google processes data according to the Google Privacy Policy. Access granted to this tool can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.
