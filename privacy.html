Privacy Policy — brainfreeze-poster

Last updated: 29 October 2026

What the tool is

brainfreeze-poster is an internal tool. It uploads short videos that we produce ourselves to our own channels and reads basic statistics of those uploads. It has no public users; only the owner of the channels signs in and authorizes it.

What data it accesses

  • YouTube (via YouTube API Services): it uploads videos to our own channel (youtube.upload) and reads the channel name and the view and like counts of our own uploads (youtube.readonly).
  • It does not access data of any other user, channel or viewer.

What data it stores

Only the video IDs, post links, upload time and view/like counts of our own uploads, in a log file on our own computer. Access tokens are stored locally with access restricted to the owner. Nothing is stored on a server.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.

Google and YouTube

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. Stored data is deleted on request.
