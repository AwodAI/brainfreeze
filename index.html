brainfreeze-poster

Internal publishing tool of the Brainfreeze channels.

Brainfreeze makes short, vertical 3D animation videos (rendered in Blender) that explain science and everyday questions. brainfreeze-poster is the small internal tool that uploads these videos to our own channels on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, and reads the public view and like counts of our own uploads.

The tool has no public users and no sign-up. Only the owner of the channels authorizes it.

Channels: YouTube @brainfreezede · Instagram @brainfreezede
